     Proposed Response:

     Hello Amr,

     Thank you for reaching out.

     Bright Pattern Agent Desktop does not currently provide a built-in auto-correct or auto-complete function for
     text that agents type in chat or email replies. The input fields in both channels use the native spell-check
     capabilities of the agent’s web browser (e.g., Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge). Agents can enable their
     browser’s spell-checker and add the required languages to receive highlighted spelling suggestions, but the
     platform itself will not automatically replace or complete words.

     If your organization requires a platform-level auto-correct feature, we can submit a Product Enhancement
     Request (PER) to our Product Management team on your behalf. Please let us know if you would like us to
     proceed and feel free to share any additional details about business impact or specific requirements so we can
     include them in the request.

     Best regards,
